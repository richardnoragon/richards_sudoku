# **📄 Rollout Plan Template**

## Development-stage evidence

When applying this template, record the candidate revision, intended stage, and
linked evidence against [alpha.md](alpha.md). Copy the actual assessment from
[PROJECT_STATUS.md](PROJECT_STATUS.md); unfilled fields and version numbers do not
prove Alpha, Beta, or release readiness.

Reusable template; fill in owners, dates, applicable checks, and evidence for each use.
This is not a completed project audit. The [versioning policy](Versioning%20%20Standard%20%20Policy.md)
and [CI enforcement plan](CI%20Enforcement%20Plan.md) define the current project scope.
API/container checks are conditional; automated version and changelog checks remain future work.

## 1. Initiative
- Name of the standard, policy, or technology being rolled out.  
- Link to governing document (e.g., Versioning Policy).

---

## 2. Rollout Owner
- Responsible steward or project lead.  
- Contact information for escalation.

---

## 3. Rollout Timeline
- **Start Date**: YYYY‑MM‑DD  
- **Target Completion Date**: YYYY‑MM‑DD  
- **Phases**: Define rollout stages (pilot, limited adoption, full adoption).  
- **Milestones**: Key checkpoints (e.g., pilot validation, 50% adoption, 100% enforcement).

---

## 4. Scope
- Teams, repositories, or systems included in rollout.  
- Dependencies on external services or APIs.  
- Exclusions (if any).

---

## 5. Rollout Phases
- **Phase 1: Pilot**  
  - Select representative teams or systems.  
  - Validate feasibility and gather feedback.  
- **Phase 2: Limited Adoption**  
  - Expand to additional teams with monitoring.  
  - Address blockers before scaling further.  
- **Phase 3: Full Adoption**  
  - Enforce across all projects.  
  - CI/CD pipelines block non‑compliant releases.  

---

## 6. Risks & Mitigations
- Identify rollout risks (e.g., resistance, tooling gaps).  
- Define mitigation strategies (training, phased enforcement, rollback paths).

---

## 7. Dependencies
- Technical dependencies (tooling, automation).  
- Organizational dependencies (approvals, cross‑team coordination).

---

## 8. Communication Plan
- Announce rollout phases and timelines.  
- Provide documentation, training, and support channels.  
- Share FAQs and migration guides.  

---

## 9. Metrics
- Define rollout success criteria (e.g., % adoption, compliance rate).  
- Track progress with measurable indicators.  

---

## 10. Blockers
- Document blockers and escalation paths.  
- Assign responsibility for resolution.  

---

## 11. Status Updates
- Provide periodic updates (weekly/bi‑weekly).  
- Include progress reports and issues encountered.  

---

## 12. Completion Criteria
- Define what “rollout complete” means (e.g., all teams compliant, CI enforcement active).  
- Steward verification required before closure.  

---

## 13. Changelog
- Track revisions to this rollout plan.  

---
